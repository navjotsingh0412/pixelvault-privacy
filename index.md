# Pixel Vault – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 9 November 2026. Contact: navproductions0412@gmail.com*

## Your files stay on your phone

Pixel Vault locks photos, videos and documents inside the app. Everything you add is encrypted on your device.

**Pixel Vault has no account, no server and no cloud.** We never receive, see, upload or share your files, file names, PIN, recovery code or backup password. They never leave your phone, except in a backup file that you create and save yourself.

## What the app stores on your device

- Your encrypted files and an encrypted list of their names and folders.
- A salted, hashed form of your PIN and recovery code (never the plain values) and your settings.
- Encryption keys, held in Android's secure hardware-backed key store.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the vault is destroyed. Use the Backup feature to keep a copy.

## Ads (the only use of the internet)

Pixel Vault is free and shows a small banner ad and, now and then, a full-screen ad (for example after you finish adding files or close a photo or video) from **Google AdMob**. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google uses this to serve and measure ads and, if you agree, to personalise them. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

- In the EU/UK and other regions where required, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested.
- You can change your ad consent at any time in **Settings → Ads → Ad choices** (shown where the law requires it).
- You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- If the phone is offline, no ad is requested.

The ads never have access to your files. The one-time purchase below removes both the banner and the full-screen ads.

## Purchases

You can pay once to remove ads. The payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**: we never see your card or payment details. Google tells the app only whether your account owns "Remove ads", so it can be restored on a new phone. We keep no account and no purchase records of our own.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state:** only to load ads, check purchases with Google Play and know whether to show the ad area.
- **Biometrics:** only if you choose fingerprint unlock. The fingerprint data stays inside Android and is never read by the app.
- **Motion sensors:** only if you turn on "Shake to lock" or "Face down to lock". The readings are used on the phone to lock the vault and are never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Vibration:** for small touch feedback, if you leave it on.
- The app has no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or all files. You pick the files you add.

## Children

Pixel Vault is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Material changes will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
